WHO DO I NOMINATE ?
To ensure that your dependents are covered, we request you to keep in mind the following:
You can nominate up to 5 dependents:
Employees and JOC
1. Father or Father-in Law*
2. Mother or Mother-in-Law*
3. Spouse
4. Upto 2 children
*Either Parents or Parents in Law can be nominated. Cross combination is not allowed. Example : You cannot select Mother and Father-in-Law
Types of updations you can make online:
Add dependents and their details in case there is a change due to marriage or birth of a child in your family.
If the details shown are incorrect or You want to delete a dependent’s details:
Please write a mail to corporatehr@timesgroup.com outlining the discrepancy and we will make the necessary change in the system.
