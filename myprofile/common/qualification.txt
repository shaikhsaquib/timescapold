<span class = "bold">How do I select an appropriate value for Qualification ?</span>

<ol>

	<li>
		Enter your Qualifications in the reverse chronological order 
		i.e. the most recent qualification should be entered first.
	</li><br><br>    


	<li>
	  Qualifications that are currently being pursued should not be entered.
	</li><br><br>

	<li>
	  Click on the <img src = "../images/but_review.jpg" border = "0"> 
	  which will open a Qualification Popup.
	</li><br><br>


	<li>
		Select your qualification from the list-box.
	</li><br><br>

	<li>
		Click on the "submit" button to post your details.
	</li><br><br>

</ol>

<span class = "bold">How do I select / enter an appropriate value for Institute ?</span>

<ol>
	<li>Click on the <img src = "../images/but_review.jpg" border = "0"> 
		which will open an Institute Popup.
	</li><br><br>
	
	<li>
		Select your Institute from the list-box.
	</li><br><br>
	
	<li>
		In case the name of your Institute is not available in the 
		list-box,enter the name of your institute in the textbox 
		available below the list-box.
	</li><br><br>
	
	<li>
		Please enter the complete name of your Institute 
		e.g. Narsee Monjee Institute Of Management Studies.
	</li><br><br>
	
	<li>
		Click on the "submit" button to post your details.
	</li><br><br>
</ol>

<span class = "bold">How do I select / enter an appropriate value for Specialization ?</span>

<ol>
	<li>
		Click on the <img src = "../images/but_review.jpg" border = "0"> 
		which will open a Specialization Popup.
	</li><br><br>
	
	<li>
		Select your Specialization from the list-box.
	</li><br><br>
	
	<li>
		In case the description of your Specialization is not available 
		in the list-box, enter your Specialization in the textbox available 
		below the list-box.
	</li><br><br>
	
	<li>
		Please enter the complete name of your Specialization 
		e.g. Post Graduate Diploma In Business Administration.
	</li><br><br>
	
	<li>
		Click on the "submit" button to post your details.
	</li><br><br>
</ol>

<span class = "bold">How do I select / enter an appropriate value for Board/University ?</span>

<ol>
	<li>
		Click on the <img src = "../images/but_review.jpg" border = "0"> 
		which will open a Board/University Popup.
	</li><br><br>
	
	<li>
		Select your Board/University from the list-box.
	</li><br><br>
	
	<li>
		In case the name of your Board/University is not available 
		in the list-box, enter your Board/University in the textbox 
		available below the list-box.
	</li><br><br>
	
	<li>
		Please enter the complete name of your Board/University as it 
		appears on your degree e.g. University of Chennai.
	</li><br><br>
	
	<li>
		Click on the "submit" button to post your details.
	</li><br><br>
</ol>
