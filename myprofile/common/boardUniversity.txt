<span class = "bold">How do I select an appropriate Board/University ?</span>

<ol>
<li>Click on the <img src = "../images/but_review.jpg" border = "0"> which will open a Board/University Popup.</li><br><br>
<li>Select your Board/University from the list-box.</li><br><br>
<li>In case the name of your Board/University is not available in the list-box, enter your Board/University in the textbox available below the list-box.</li><br><br>
<li>Please enter the complete name of your Board/University as it appears on your degree e.g. University of Chennai.</li> <br><br>
<li>Click on the "submit" button to post your details.</li><br><br>
</ol>
