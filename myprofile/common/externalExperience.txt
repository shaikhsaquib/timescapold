<span class = "bold">What do I mention in Company Name ?</span><br><br>

<p align = "justify" style="line-height: 150%">
<ol>
<li>Enter the full name of the Company for which you were working e.g. Silverline Technologies.</li><br><br>
<li>Please provide details in reverse chronological order (Last Company name should appear first).<br><br>
<li>In case you have worked with any Company within Timesgroup e.g. Times Internet Ltd, please mention details of you employment under Employment History - Previous Organizations.</li><br><br>
</ol>
</p>

<span class = "bold">What do I enter / select as an appropriate value for Designation ?</span><br><br>

<p align = "justify" style="line-height: 150%">
<ol>
<li>  Mention details of the Designation at which you joined the company as the value for Joined As. </li><br><br>

<li>  Mention details about the Last Designation held by you while leaving the company.</li><br><br>

</ol>
</p>

<ol>
