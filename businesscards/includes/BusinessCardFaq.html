FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
 
BUSINESS CARD APPROVAL
 
For the Requestor :


How do I request for a Business Card ?
 

The Create Request link will lead you to put in the request for a Business Card.
The name, designation, address, location are editable fields and can be changed by you but will go to HR for verification.
For eg. a person with the name Ramakrishnan Setaraman Balasubramanian may want his name to appear as
R.S. Balasubramanian on his Business Card.

 

How do I choose my design template for the Business card ?
 

The system by default will showcase the relevant template according to your department. But still in case you belong to a particular Brand within a department, you may have to choose your required template.

For eg. A person who belongs to Response department will get the Response department template by default. But he may belong to the Times Red Cell which is within Response. In such a case, he will have to go and choose the relevant template in the drop down.

 


What is the work flow once I request for a Business Card ?
 

When you put a request in "create request" you can view the status in the "check status" as "In Process". Your immediate superior who is your approver gets the request and may approve based on all the information edited by you.Then the status changes to "Appoved". In case you have made changes in the name, designation or address, then it gets reviewed by HR department too. The status thus changes to "Reviewed". The next step is of the Processor who sends it out to the vendor for printing. The status changes to "Processed". Once the vendor is through with the printing, he dispatches the Business card to you. Once that is done, the status changes to "Dispatched". When you get the printed Business Cards in hand, go to the "close request" link and close the request. This gives the indication that you have received the finished product.

 

Can I cancel any request?
 

Yes, you can cancel the request once you submit it, till your request reaches the "Processed" status. Once it goes to the vendor, you cannot cancel your Business Card request.

 
What do I do when my request gets rejected ?
 
In case your request gets rejected, you will be able to view the reason for rejection in the remarks field under Check Status.
The same request cannot be rectified, but you will have to put in a fresh request with the required modifications.

 
 
For the Approver:


What are the actions I can take as an approver ?
 

When a request has been sent to you, you can view the details in the "process request". At this point the status shows "In Process". When you get the request you can view the details of the requestor. Check the card template and details before approving. In case the requestor has edited any fields, it will be visible in red. You can approve the request or reject it. The rejection has to be supported by your comments in the remarks field. In case the requestor has edited his name, designation and address; after your approval it will be further reviewed by HR. Only the reviewed and approved requests will go to the Processor.
