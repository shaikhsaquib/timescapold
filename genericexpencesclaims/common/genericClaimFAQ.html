FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
 
GENERIC CLAIM QUERIES
 

Do I need to submit a printout of the Claim to the Finance Department even after submitting my Generic Claim online?

According to rules of the Income Tax Department there is a need for a physical proof for any kind of reimbursement that the Organisation provides for their employees.

Due to this rule, the Finance Department will require the printout of the Claim submitted by you online. After you fill in the Claim, click on the "Check Status" link to view the Transaction ID of the Claim. By clicking on the Transaction ID you get the details of the Claim submitted. The print option is provided on the top right hand corner of this page. Print his page and add your signature and date before submitting it to the Finance Department along with all the bills attached.

 


How do I benefit from submitting my Generic Claim online?

With the Online ESS system on Timescape, the employee is able to check his balance Claim as well as have a record of all the Claims submitted to the Finance Department with the details.

 
 
Saving As Draft
 

How do I file my Bills and submit at a later stage?

In order to do that, after you have added the list of bills - click on the Preview button and view the list. Next, click the Save as Draft button to save it as a draft (that means it is not submitted)
At a later stage you can go to the Check Status page and edit your bill list once again to add more bills to it. Once its submitted you cannot edit it anymore. Instead you can create a new bill list, incase you have more to add.

 

What do I do if I have more than five bills to submit, since there are only five rows to enter ?

There is an additional button at the side "Add More Bills" which allows you to save the current list you have entered as a draft and then adds more rows so that you can enter more bills.
